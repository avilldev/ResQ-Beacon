# 🚨 ResQ-Beacon

An AI-powered disaster relief coordination platform designed and developed to replace chaotic emergency dispatch queues by allowing victims to submit lightweight, direct situation reports that are instantly analyzed, categorized, and prioritized by a local artificial intelligence model for emergency responders.

## Setup Guide
Refer to ResQ-Beacon-Two-Computers-Setup-Guide.txt in the repository root.

## Our Mission
Helping people affected by disasters connect with emergency resources and assistance.
